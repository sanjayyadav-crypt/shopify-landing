A streamlined, transparent workflow designed to keep you in the loop at every stage.

01

Discovery & Scoping

We deep-dive into your requirements, business goals, and technical constraints to deliver a detailed project scope, timeline, and fixed-cost estimate.

02

Architecture & Design

Our team designs the app architecture, database schema, and UX flows — aligned with Shopify's design system before any code is written.
