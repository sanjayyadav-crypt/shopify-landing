Shopify Plus gives you full access to Checkout Extensibility — the only plan that can modify the checkout experience beyond the basics. Shopify Plus vs Standard

Is Shopify Plus Right for Your Business?

Shopify Plus starts at $2,300/month. Here's exactly what you get that you can't get on standard Shopify — and when it's worth the upgrade.

Feature Shopify Standard Shopify Plus ✦
Checkout Customisation Limited — you can change colours and logo but cannot add custom fields, UI extensions, or logic to the checkout flow Full Checkout Extensibility — custom UI extensions, checkout functions, upsell blocks, gift messages, loyalty redemption, and B2B fields. Plus only
B2B & Wholesale No native B2B — you need third-party apps to approximate wholesale pricing, company accounts, and net payment terms Native B2B — company accounts, custom price lists per company, purchase orders, net terms, and assigned sales reps built in. Native B2B
Shopify Markets Basic multi-currency — limited localisation, no local domains per country, no market-specific pricing rules Full Shopify Markets — per-market pricing, currencies, languages, domains, duty-inclusive checkout, and market-level app configuration. Full Markets

Shopify Flow & Automations Basic automations only — limited triggers and actions, no complex multi-step workflows or cross-system automation Advanced Shopify Flow — complex multi-step automations, custom triggers, third-party app triggers, and Launchpad for campaign scheduling. Advanced Flow
Staff Accounts Limited staff accounts (2–15 depending on plan) — problematic for larger teams Unlimited staff accounts with granular permission controls — ideal for enterprise teams with multiple departments and roles. Unlimited staff

Shopify Plus Development — Questions Answered

Shopify Plus is Shopify's enterprise plan starting at $2,300/month. It's worth upgrading when you need: custom checkout modifications (checkout extensibility), a native B2B portal, advanced Shopify Markets for multiple countries, zero transaction fees at scale, unlimited staff accounts, or Shopify Flow for advanced automations. A common rule of thumb: if you're doing $1M+/year in revenue, the checkout conversion gains and fee savings typically pay for Plus within months.
Shopify Plus unlocks features that require specific development expertise: Checkout Extensibility (custom UI extensions inside the checkout), Shopify Functions (custom discount and shipping logic), native B2B portal configuration and customisation, Shopify Markets at an enterprise level, Launchpad for campaign automation, and Shopify Flow for complex multi-step automations. A Plus agency has deep experience building and configuring all of these — a standard Shopify agency typically doesn't.
Yes — enterprise migrations to Shopify Plus are one of our core services. We handle all data migration (products, customers, orders, SEO URLs), rebuild custom functionality in Shopify Plus, and manage the cutover with zero downtime and zero SEO ranking loss.

Enterprise migrations to Shopify Plus take 8–20 weeks depending on data complexity. Headless Hydrogen builds take 12–20 weeks. We provide an accurate timeline in your scoping proposal before you commit.
Yes — Shopify Plus B2B is one of our specialities. Yes — Hydrogen headless development on Shopify Plus is a core service we offer. We build Hydrogen storefronts using React and Remix, deployed on Shopify's Oxygen edge infrastructure.
