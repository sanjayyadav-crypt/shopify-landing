Common Squarespace Limitations

The Squarespace Limits Shopify Removes Permanently

Squarespace is perfect for getting started — beautiful templates, easy setup, all-in-one hosting. But these are the exact limitations that push growing stores to make the switch.

🔌

Tiny App Ecosystem

Squarespace has around 30 extensions — Shopify has 8,000+. Every advanced feature you need — subscriptions, loyalty programmes, returns management, upsells — is a workaround on Squarespace and a one-click install on Shopify.

🛒

Locked Checkout Experience

Squarespace checkout cannot be customised. No gift message fields, no loyalty redemption, no custom upsell blocks — you get what Squarespace gives you. Shopify's checkout is the most customisable and highest-converting in eCommerce.

📦

Product Catalogue Limits

Squarespace becomes unwieldy with large catalogues. Complex variant structures, bulk editing, and inventory management at scale are all significantly more capable in Shopify — built from the ground up for product-first businesses.

🔄

No Native Subscriptions

If you sell subscription products, Squarespace offers no native recurring billing. You need expensive workarounds. Shopify has native subscription support plus ReCharge, Bold, and other best-in-class subscription apps — all ready to go.

📊

Limited Analytics

Squarespace analytics are basic — you can see traffic and sales, but not cohort analysis, LTV, product performance, or inventory forecasting. Shopify's analytics give you everything a growing eCommerce business needs to make good decisions.

🌍

Weak International Commerce

Multi-currency on Squarespace is limited and finicky. Shopify Markets handles multi-country, multi-currency, and multi-language commerce from a single admin — with localised pricing, checkout, and domains built in.
